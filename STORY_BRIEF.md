# JUMPSHOT: game brief for story writing

## The pitch
JUMPSHOT is a fast 2D stickman combo-fighter for phones and browsers. You flick on the screen and your stickman dashes in and strikes on its own. The signature move is the **Jumpshot**: an uppercut that launches an enemy into the sky. You leap after them, juggle them in mid-air, then spike them back into the floor. In Rhythm Mode the music is the clock: every hit snaps to the beat, and the enemies attack on the beat too.

## The look and the world
- Everything happens **on paper**. The main "Pixel" look is black ink on white, with only two accent colours: **blue** for the hero and **red** for enemies.
- The fighters are hand-drawn stick figures, animated like a flipbook. Hits flash with comic-book impact frames.
- The arenas are pages:
  - the **Blank Page**;
  - a **Lined Notebook**;
  - **Graph Paper**;
  - a **Sketchbook**;
  - brown **Kraft Paper** for boss fights.
- There is also a "neon" look, which shows the same world glowing on a dark screen.
- **Story hook:** the whole game is a doodle come to life inside someone's notebook. The hero is an ink drawing that fights other drawings, page after page.

## The hero
- A blue-banded stickman. The player customises the hero in the **Home** screen:
  - **Headbands, headgear and hand gear.** More cosmetics are planned: capes, scarves, belts and weapon skins.
  - **Two trait slots**, such as Iron Skin, Glass Cannon, Adrenaline, Lightfoot (an extra air jump), Steady Nerves (better parries), Vampire (life steal), **Laser Eyes**, and Executioner.
- A **Training Room** has a straw practice **Dummy** and a **Punching Bag**.

## Weapons (each one has its own moveset and a cinematic Special)
- **Fists:** strings, uppercuts, spikes.
- **Staff:** spins, a boomerang throw, a pole-vault kick.
- **Laser Blaster:** beams and blasts.
- **Grappling Hook:** swing along the ceiling and zip to walls, Spider-Man style.
- **Whip.**
- **Hammer:** shockwaves. Its Special is EARTHSPLITTER.
- **Daggers:** smoke poofs and blinks. Its Special is BLADE STORM, which stops time.
- **Ice Spear:** ice walls, crystal spikes and frost floors. Its Special is DEEP FREEZE: enemies are encased in ice, the hero points, and everything SHATTERS.
- **Scythe:** souls and doom marks. Its Special is REAPER, a giant shadow scythe that drains the world to black and white.
- **Ray Gun.**
- **Last-hit KO finishers:** each weapon has a cinematic kill, for example the Ice Spear lunge that impales and shatters, and the Hammer overhead slam.

## Enemies (red ink drawings)
- **Grunt:** a basic brawler.
- **Rusher:** fast and lunges at you.
- **Kicker:** a martial artist.
- **Thrower:** keeps its distance and throws things.
- **Brute:** big, heavy and slow, with a ground slam.
- **Shield:** blocks from the front, so you have to go around or under.
- **RONIN:** an elite swordsman-style rival with a scarf and full combos.

## Bosses (one every few waves, on kraft paper)
- **THE COLOSSUS:** a giant horned brute. Meteors rain down during the fight and it summons grunts.
- **SCRIBBLA, The Ink Witch:** a witch in a hat who teleports and summons.
- **THE JUGGERNAUT:** an armoured charger. You have to dodge the charge.
- **SHADOW, Your Ink Double:** a dark copy of the hero that dodges like you do.

## Structure
- **Waves:** you fight through waves of enemies, with a boss cycle across 40 waves (see the Roadmap screen).
- **Scoring:** comboing, juggling and parrying earn style ranks.
- **Special Bar:** fills as you fight. Hold still to trigger it, then trace a shape for a big cinematic attack.

## Tone
Kinetic, playful and comic-book loud: "SHATTER!", "JUMPSHOT!", "PERFECT PARRY". It is a hand-drawn underdog doodle taking on everything the page throws at it.
